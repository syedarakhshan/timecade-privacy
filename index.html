Privacy Policy for TimeCade

Last updated: November 16, 2026

TimeCade ("we", "our", "the app") is a timetable management app for universities. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your university login ID (Teacher ID or JUW student ID) and password, used to sign in.
  • Timetable data: courses, schedules, and related details shown and managed in the app.
  • Chatbot messages: messages users send to the in-app AI assistant, which are processed to generate replies.

How We Use Information

We use this information only to provide app features such as signing in, showing timetables, and answering questions through the chatbot. We do not use it for advertising.

Third-Party Services

App data is stored in a PostgreSQL database on our server. Chatbot messages are sent to the Qwen API (Alibaba Cloud) to generate replies. This service has its own privacy policy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data is shared only with the service provider listed above (Qwen API), as needed to run the chatbot.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data is kept while the account is active. Users can request deletion of their data as described below.

Account Deletion

To request deletion of your TimeCade account and associated data (login ID, timetable data, and chatbot messages), send an email to noorsyeda349@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete TimeCade account", mentioning your login ID. We will delete your account and data within 30 days of receiving your request.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. We take reasonable steps to protect data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

TimeCade is not directed at children under 13.
